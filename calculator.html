How the calculator works

Tap a talent to see what this rank and the next one give, then use + and − to spend points (on a computer, click adds a point, right-click removes one, and hovering shows a quick tooltip). Rows open every 5 points in a tree; lines mean the earlier talent must be maxed. The order you spend points is saved in the build link as a leveling order.
