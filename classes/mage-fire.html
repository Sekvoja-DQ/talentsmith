All Fire talents

Tap a level to see its talents with their in-game text.

Level 10+2
  • Incineration Level 10 · 5 ranks

    Fire Blast, Ice Lance, Arcane Blast and Scorch get +10% critical strike chance.

  • Accelerant Level 10 · 5 ranks

    Fireball, Frostfire Bolt and Arcane Wrath cast 0.5 sec faster.

Level 15+3
  • Ignite Level 15 · 5 ranks

    Fire spell critical strikes, including Flamestrike's damage after its first hit, burn the target for up to an extra 40% of the damage over 4 sec.

  • Flame Throwing Level 15 · 2 ranks

    Fire spells get +6 yd range.

  • Impact Level 15 · 3 ranks

    Fire spells have a 10% chance to stun the target for 2 sec.

Level 20+4
  • Burning Soul Level 20 · 3 ranks

    A 70% chance that taking damage doesn't delay your Fire spells, and Fire spells cause 30% less threat.

  • Improved Flamestrike Level 20 · 3 ranks

    Flamestrike gets +15% critical strike chance.

  • Pyroblast Level 20 · 1 rank

    6 sec cast: 101–131 Fire damage plus 44 over 12 sec. 125 mana, 35 yd.

  • Improved Fire Blast Level 20 · 2 ranks

    Fire Blast's cooldown is 2 sec shorter and its range 15 yd longer.

Level 25+4
  • Improved Scorch Level 25 · 3 ranks

    Scorch has a 100% chance to make the target take 3% more Fire damage for 30 sec, stacking 5 times.

  • Improved Fire Ward Level 25 · 2 ranks

    Fire Ward has a 20% chance to reflect Fire spells.

  • Heating Up Level 25 · 1 rank

    Direct crits from Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast and Scorch make your next Pyroblast within 20 sec cast 25% faster, stacking 3 times; Fire Blast adds an extra stack whenever it deals damage.

  • Master of Elements Level 25 · 3 ranks

    Fire and Frost spell critical strikes refund 30% of the spell's base mana cost.

Level 30+2
  • Critical Mass Level 30 · 3 ranks

    +6% critical strike chance with Fire spells.

  • Blast Wave Level 30 · 1 rank

    Instant: 154–184 Fire damage to nearby enemies, slowing them by 50% (Dazed) for 6 sec. 215 mana, 45 sec cooldown.

Level 35+1
  • Fire Power Level 35 · 3 ranks

    Your Fire spells deal 3% more damage for each of your Fire effects on the target, +12%.

Level 40+1
  • Combustion Level 40 · 1 rank

    Each Fire spell hit adds +10% Fire critical strike chance until 3 non-periodic Fire crits have landed. 3 min cooldown.
