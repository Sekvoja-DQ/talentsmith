All Frost talents

Tap a level to see its talents with their in-game text.

Level 10+3
  • Frost Warding Level 10 · 2 ranks

    Frost Armor and Ice Armor give 30% more armor and resistance, Frost Ward has a 20% chance to negate the spell it absorbs, and Ice Barrier and Frost Ward absorb 20% more.

  • Improved Frostbolt Level 10 · 5 ranks

    Frostbolt casts 0.5 sec faster.

  • Precision Level 10 · 3 ranks

    +6% hit chance with all spells.

Level 15+4
  • Ice Shards Level 15 · 5 ranks

    Frost spell critical strikes deal 100% more bonus damage.

  • Frostbite Level 15 · 3 ranks

    Your Chill effects have a 15% chance to freeze the target for 5 sec. The freeze shares one diminishing-returns group with Entrapment, Counterattack, Improved Wing Clip and Improved Hamstring.

  • Improved Frost Nova Level 15 · 2 ranks

    Frost Nova's cooldown is 4 sec shorter.

  • Permafrost Level 15 · 3 ranks

    Chill effects last 33% longer and slow the target by up to another 10%.

Level 20+3
  • Frost Channeling Level 20 · 3 ranks

    Frost spells cost 15% less mana and cause 30% less threat.

  • Improved Blizzard Level 20 · 3 ranks

    Blizzard also Chills targets, slowing them by 40% for 1.5 sec.

  • Ice Lance Level 20 · 1 rank

    Instant: 28–32 Frost damage, 300% more against Frozen targets. 45 mana, 30 yd, 8 sec cooldown shared with Fire Blast.

Level 25+3
  • Arctic Reach Level 25 · 2 ranks

    Frostbolt, Ice Lance and Blizzard get 20% more range, and Frost Nova and Cone of Cold 20% more radius.

  • Ice Block Level 25 · 1 rank

    Immune to physical attacks and spells for 10 sec, but you can't attack, move or cast. 5 min cooldown.

  • Shatter Level 25 · 3 ranks

    +50% critical strike chance with all spells against Frozen targets.

Level 30+3
  • Improved Cone of Cold Level 30 · 3 ranks

    Cone of Cold deals 35% more damage.

  • Cold Snap Level 30 · 1 rank

    Finishes the cooldown of your other Frost spells. 10 min cooldown.

  • Fingers of Frost Level 30 · 2 ranks

    Your Chill effects have a 15% chance to treat your next 2 spells (1 at rank 1) as if the target were Frozen. Lasts 15 sec.

Level 35+1
  • Winter's Chill Level 35 · 5 ranks

    Frostbolt and Fingers of Frost Ice Lances have a 100% chance to apply Winter's Chill, stacking 5 times: each stack makes your next Cone of Cold deal 20% more damage plus 7% of your spell damage.

Level 40+1
  • Ice Barrier Level 40 · 1 rank

    Absorbs 448 damage plus 10% of your Frost spell power and raises your Frost damage by 15% for 1 min, plus another 5% while the shield holds; damage doesn't delay your casts while shielded. 305 mana, 30 sec cooldown.
