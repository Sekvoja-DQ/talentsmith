All Holy talents

Tap a level to see its talents with their in-game text.

Level 10+2
  • Spiritual Focus Level 10 · 5 ranks

    A 70% chance that taking damage doesn't slow your Flash of Light, Holy Light and Light's Vigil casts.

  • Divine Strength Level 10 · 5 ranks

    10% more Strength.

Level 15+4
  • Healing Light Level 15 · 3 ranks

    Holy Light, Flash of Light and Holy Shock heal 12% more.

  • Reverence Level 15 · 2 ranks

    30% of your mana regeneration continues while casting.

  • Improved Seals Level 15 · 5 ranks

    Your Seals and Judgements deal 15% more damage.

  • Purifying Power Level 15 · 2 ranks

    Cleanse and Purify cost 20% less mana, and Exorcism and Holy Wrath cooldowns are 33% shorter.

Level 20+3
  • Divine Favor Level 20 · 1 rank

    Your next Flash of Light, Holy Light or Holy Shock heal is a guaranteed critical strike. 2 min cooldown.

  • Illumination Level 20 · 5 ranks

    After a critical heal, a 100% chance to get back 50% of the spell's base mana cost. Also triggers from Light's Vigil.

  • Holy Conduit Level 20 · 2 ranks

    Consecration, Holy Wrath, Exorcism and Hammer of Wrath cost 40% less mana.

Level 25+3
  • Divine Precision Level 25 · 2 ranks

    8% hit chance with Holy Shock, Exorcism, Consecration, Holy Wrath, Holy Strike, Hammer of Justice, Repentance, Turn Evil and Light's Vigil (not Judgements, Seal of Command or Hammer of Wrath), and silences on you are 20% shorter.

  • Infusion of Light Level 25 · 2 ranks

    Holy Shock and Flash of Light crits cut your next Holy Light's cast time by 1 sec.

  • Oath of Light Level 25 · 1 rank

    Swear an Oath on one ally for 5 min: your Holy Light heals them for 400 more, Flash of Light for 115 more and Holy Shock for 230 more. One Oath at a time; 8% of base mana, 40 yd, instant.

Level 30+3
  • Consecrated Ground Level 30 · 2 ranks

    Your Holy spells deal 10% more damage to the first 4 enemies that enter your Consecration.

  • Holy Shock Level 30 · 1 rank

    Instantly damages an enemy or heals an ally with Holy power. 10 sec cooldown.

  • Boundless Light Level 30 · 2 ranks

    30% more critical strike chance for Holy Shock on allies that don't have your Oath.

Level 35+1
  • Holy Power Level 35 · 5 ranks

    Critical heals from your Holy spells heal 50% more.

Level 40+1
  • Light's Vigil Level 40 · 1 rank

    Grants Vigil to a friendly target for 30 sec (40 yd): your next Holy Shock on that ally skips the cooldown and heals them and their party.
