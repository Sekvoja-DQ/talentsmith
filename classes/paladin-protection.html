All Protection talents

Tap a level to see its talents with their in-game text.

Level 10+2
  • Divine Intellect Level 10 · 5 ranks

    10% more Intellect.

  • Redoubt Level 10 · 5 ranks

    Damaging melee hits against you have a 10% chance to raise your block chance by 30% for 10 sec or 5 blocks.

Level 15+3
  • Precision Level 15 · 3 ranks

    +3% hit chance with melee attacks and +3% with spells.

  • Guardian's Favor Level 15 · 2 ranks

    Blessing of Protection's cooldown is 2 min shorter and Blessing of Freedom lasts 6 sec longer.

  • Sacred Duty Level 15 · 2 ranks

    Divine Shield, Divine Protection and Templar's Bulwark cooldowns are 60 sec shorter, and Forbearance is 10 sec shorter.

Level 20+4
  • Templar's Bulwark Level 20 · 1 rank

    An absorb shield equal to 100% of your max health for 8 sec. It is a Magic effect and can be dispelled. Applies Forbearance for 1 min. 5 min cooldown.

  • Improved Hammer of Justice Level 20 · 3 ranks

    Hammer of Justice's cooldown is 15 sec shorter.

  • Shield Specialization Level 20 · 2 ranks

    Blocks absorb 30% more and have a 100% chance to restore 4% mana, at most once every 3 sec.

  • Toughness Level 20 · 5 ranks

    10% more armor from items.

Level 25+3
  • Swift Judgement Level 25 · 1 rank

    Unleashes your Seal's Judgement without using up the Seal, on its own cooldown.

  • Improved Righteous Fury Level 25 · 3 ranks

    While Righteous Fury is active you take 9% less damage.

  • Anticipation Level 25 · 5 ranks

    +20 Defense skill.

Level 30+3
  • Stoicism Level 30 · 2 ranks

    Stun and Charm effects on you are 20% shorter.

  • Holy Shield Level 30 · 1 rank

    Increases block chance by 30% and deals Holy damage when you block. 10 sec cooldown.

  • Reckoning Level 30 · 5 ranks

    After you block (a 40% chance) or take a non-periodic critical hit (100%) you gain an extra attack; stores up to 4 charges, at most one from blocks every 1.5 sec.

Level 35+1
  • Iron Creed Level 35 · 3 ranks

    30% more Holy Strike threat and 6% less damage taken.

Level 40+1
  • Shieldsworn Level 40 · 1 rank

    Holy Strike's cooldown is 4 sec shorter and it hits with your shield, triggering Holy Shield and Shield Specialization without using their charges. Also blocks spells for 6 sec.
