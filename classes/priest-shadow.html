All Shadow talents

Tap a level to see its talents with their in-game text.

Level 10+3
  • Shadow Focus Level 10 · 5 ranks

    +5% hit chance with Shadow spells.

  • Blackout Level 10 · 5 ranks

    Shadow damage spells have a 10% chance to stun for 3 sec.

  • Spirit Tap Level 10 · 5 ranks

    After a kill (or when a target with your Vampiric Embrace dies) you have a 100% chance that your Spirit doubles for 15 sec and 50% of your mana regeneration continues while casting.

Level 15+3
  • Improved Mind Blast Level 15 · 5 ranks

    Mind Blast's cooldown is 2.5 sec shorter.

  • Improved Shadow Word: Pain Level 15 · 2 ranks

    Shadow Word: Pain lasts 6 sec longer.

  • Shadow Reach Level 15 · 2 ranks

    Offensive Shadow spells get 20% more range.

Level 20+3
  • Improved Psychic Scream Level 20 · 2 ranks

    Psychic Scream's cooldown is 4 sec shorter.

  • Mind Flay Level 20 · 1 rank

    Channel: 63 Shadow damage over 3 sec and slows the target by 50%. 45 mana, 20 yd.

  • Improved Mind Flay Level 20 · 2 ranks

    Each Mind Flay tick deals 20% more than the one before.

Level 25+3
  • Dark Inspiration Level 25 · 5 ranks

    Mind Blast and fully channeled Mind Flay raise your party's Spirit (and yours) by 15% for 10 sec.

  • Vampiric Embrace Level 25 · 1 rank

    For 30 sec your party is healed for 10% of your Shadow spell damage on the target, and you for 20%; this healing cannot crit. 30 sec cooldown.

  • Shadow Weaving Level 25 · 3 ranks

    Your Shadow spells have a 100% chance to make the target take 2% more Shadow damage for 15 sec, stacking 5 times.

Level 30+2
  • Silence Level 30 · 1 rank

    Silences the target for 5 sec (interrupts for 3 sec). 45 sec cooldown.

  • Devouring Contagion Level 30 · 2 ranks

    Devouring Plague costs 50% less mana, and a target that dies with it spreads it to 2 enemies within 10 yd (critters don't count).

Level 35+2
  • Early Demise Level 35 · 2 ranks

    Shadow Word: Death gets +30% crit chance on targets at or below 20% health.

  • Darkness Level 35 · 5 ranks

    Your Shadow spells deal 10% more damage.

Level 40+1
  • Shadowform Level 40 · 1 rank

    Shadow damage +10%, Shadow spells cost 50% less mana and their crits deal +100% bonus damage, physical damage taken −15%; no healing spells while active.
