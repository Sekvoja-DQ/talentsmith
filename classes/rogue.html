Rogue talents changed for launch

TalentTreeNow
Improved Expose ArmorAssassinationExpose Armor costs up to 10 less Energy and, at 5 combo points, gives up to 2 combo points back. Moved up to row 2.
VigorAssassinationUp to +10 maximum Energy. Moved up to row 3.
Blood SplatterAssassinationRupture lasts 6 sec longer and Garrote lasts 9 sec longer.
LethalityAssassinationUp to +20% critical damage bonus (10% per rank) on Sinister Strike, Gouge, Backstab, Mutilate, Ghostly Strike and Hemorrhage. Now 2 ranks.
Relentless StrikesAssassinationEach combo point spent on a finishing move gives up to a 20% chance (4% per rank) to get 25 Energy back. Now 5 ranks, moved to row 5.
VenomAssassinationPassive: Shank makes your poisons deal 30% more damage and apply 20% more often for 6 sec. No longer a finishing move and no longer needs Mutilate; moved up to row 5.
Seal FateAssassinationCrits from combo-point abilities have up to a 100% chance (33 / 66 / 100%) to add an extra combo point. Now 3 ranks.
MutilateAssassinationDagger attack with both weapons: 90% weapon damage plus a larger flat bonus per weapon (the new amount isn't published). 20% more damage to poisoned targets. 2 combo points. Now the 31-point talent in row 7.
Coup de GraceCombatEviscerate deals up to 20% more damage (7 / 13 / 20%), and Death Mark deals up to 20% more and stores damage up to 20% faster. Renamed from Improved Eviscerate.
Efficient StrikesCombatSinister Strike and Hemorrhage cost up to 5 less Energy (3 / 5). Renamed from Improved Sinister Strike; now also covers Hemorrhage.
Improved SprintCombatSprint has up to a 100% chance to remove movement-slowing effects. Moved up to row 2.
ComposureCombatPoised lowers the cost of your next Energy ability by up to 10 more (5 / 10). Poised is a new level-18 Rogue ability: out of combat at full Energy, your next Energy ability costs 10 less.
Flawless ExecutionCombatEviscerate costs 15 less Energy (was 10).
AggressionCombatSinister Strike and Eviscerate deal up to 15% more damage (5 / 10 / 15%); Backstab is no longer included. Moved up to row 4.
Weapon ExpertiseCombatYour attacks are up to 2% less likely to be dodged or parried. Moved up to row 4, so it no longer follows Blade Flurry.
Dual Wield SpecializationCombatOff-hand damage up to +50% (25 / 50%). Now 2 ranks, moved to row 5.
Hack and SlashCombatBy weapon type, up to: Axe/Sword 5% chance for an extra attack, Dagger/Fist +5% crit, Mace ignores 15% armor. Same effect; placed in column 4 here because Dual Wield Specialization moved into row 5.
Flash of SteelCombatShank gives up to 15 extra Energy (3 per rank) and its cooldown is up to 2 sec shorter (0.4 sec per rank). Shank is a new level-24 Rogue ability: off-hand strike for 50% weapon damage that can't miss, 5 Energy, gives back 15 Energy, 10 sec cooldown.
SetupSubtletyUp to a 45% chance (15 / 30 / 45%) to gain a combo point when you dodge an attack or fully resist a spell. Moved up to row 1.
OpportunitySubtletyBackstab, Garrote, Ambush and Mutilate deal up to 20% more damage (4% per rank). Now 5 ranks.
CamouflageSubtletyStealth slows you up to 15% less (5 / 10 / 15%) and its cooldown is up to 6 sec shorter. Now 3 ranks, moved to row 2.
Dirty DeedsSubtletyCheap Shot and Garrote cost up to 20 less Energy, and Garrote works from the front. Moved up to row 2.
PremeditationSubtletyAdds 2 combo points to a target from Stealth; use them within 20 sec. 2 min cooldown. Moved up to row 3.
Serrated BladesSubtletyAttacks ignore up to 9% of target armor and Rupture deals up to 30% more damage. Moved up to row 3.
ElusivenessSubtletyVanish and Blind cooldowns up to 90 sec shorter. Moved down to row 4.
Ghostly StrikeSubtlety125% weapon damage, and your next Ambush or Garrote within 7 sec can be used without Stealth. 1 combo point, 40 Energy, 20 sec cooldown. Moved to row 4. The extra damage with daggers was a bug and is fixed; the notes don't say whether the dodge bonus stays.
Quick GetawaySubtletySap can hold one more target, and after you Sap something you move up to 30% faster (15 / 30%) for 3 sec.
Heightened SensesSubtletySee stealth as if up to 3 levels higher, and up to 4% less likely to be hit by spells and ranged attacks. Moved down to row 5.
HemorrhageSubtlety100% weapon damage; the target takes 15% more Rupture and Garrote damage from you for 15 sec. 1 combo point, 35 Energy. Garrote is new; the extra damage with daggers was a bug and is fixed.
Boot KnifeSubtletyShank has up to a 100% chance (20% per rank) to add 2 combo points to the target.
Death MarkSubtletyCurse for 25 Energy (20 yd, 10 sec cooldown): for 6 sec it stores part of the damage you deal for each combo point (Garrote and Rupture store at double rate), then deals the stored damage, capped by your Attack Power and crit chance.
