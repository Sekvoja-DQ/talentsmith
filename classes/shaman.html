Shaman talents changed for launch

TalentTreeNow
ConvectionElementalShocks, Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning cost up to 10% less mana. (Lava Burst is removed.)
Elemental AlacrityElementalLightning Bolt and Chain Lightning cast up to 0.5 sec faster. (Lava Burst is removed.)
Call of FlameElementalFire totems, Flame Shock and Fire Nova deal up to 15% more damage, and Flame Shock lasts up to 9 sec longer. (Lava Burst is removed.)
Eye of the StormElementalUp to a 70% chance that taking damage doesn't delay Lightning Bolt or Chain Lightning. (Lava Burst is removed.)
Elemental ReachElementalLightning Bolt, Chain Lightning and Fire Nova get up to +6 yd range. No longer shortens the totem-moving spell (now called Totemic Call, 30 sec cooldown).
Call of ThunderElementalLightning Bolt and Chain Lightning get +5% critical strike chance. Replaces Lava Burst, which is removed.
Lightning OverloadElementalLightning Bolt and Chain Lightning have up to a 30% chance (10 / 20 / 30%) to fire a second copy at the same target for half damage, free and with no threat.
Lightning MasteryElementalFor 15 sec: Lightning Bolt casts 0.5 sec faster and Chain Lightning has no cooldown. 3 min cooldown. Renamed from Elemental Mastery now that Lava Burst is gone; we assume it follows Call of Thunder, which took Lava Burst's slot.
Mental DexterityEnhancementGain Attack Power equal to up to 150% of your Intellect (50 / 100 / 150%).
FlurryEnhancementAfter a melee crit your next 3 swings are up to 30% faster (6% per rank).
StormstrikeEnhancementInstant strike for 150% normalized weapon damage; your next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Earth Shock within 12 sec deals 20% more damage. 8% of base mana, 6 sec cooldown.
Improved Ghost WolfEnhancementGhost Wolf casts up to 3 sec faster and works indoors. Moved down to row 4.
Maelstrom WeaponEnhancementMelee hits can grant a stack (up to 5, 30 sec) that cuts your next Lightning Bolt's cast time and mana cost, up to 20%. Now triggers twice as often (exact chance not published).
RiptideRestorationHeals the target for 445 over 15 sec – the instant heal is gone – and Chain Heal on that target is 25% stronger. About 40% less mana, 12 sec cooldown.
