Key Arms talents

Bloodthrill 2 ranks

Main-hand hits on targets with your Rend have up to a 20% chance (10 / 20%) to enable Overpower.

Sweeping Strikes 1 rank

Battle Stance: your next 5 melee attacks also hit one more nearby enemy. 30 Rage, 30 sec cooldown.

Weaponmaster 5 ranks

Bonus by weapon type, up to: Axe/Polearm +5% crit chance, Mace/Staff ignore 15% of target armor, Sword 5% chance for an extra attack.

Improved Slam 2 ranks

Slam's cast time and global cooldown are up to 0.5 sec shorter and it doesn't interrupt or delay your melee swing. Its 15 sec cooldown is up to 5 sec shorter (2.5 / 5 sec).

Improved Hamstring 3 ranks

Hamstring has up to a 15% chance to root the target for 5 sec. The root shares one diminishing-returns group with Entrapment, Counterattack, Improved Wing Clip and Frostbite.

Mortal Strike 1 rank

Weapon damage plus 85, and the target receives 50% less healing for 10 sec. 30 Rage, 6 sec cooldown.
