WoW Forever class hotfixes – November 10, 2026

Blizzard's first class tuning: Enhancement Shaman goes back to Strength and Windfury, Affliction's Wrack now speeds up your DoTs, and Hunter and Warlock pets change. Live in the US on Nov 10 and in Europe after the Nov 11 restart. Every number below is from Blizzard's hotfix notes; the talent calculator already shows the new values.

Heads-up from players in game: Warlock tooltips already show the new text, but the Enhancement Shaman talent tooltips still show the old descriptions – the changes themselves are live.

Shaman calculator Warlock calculator Hunter calculator

What it means

  • Enhancement Shaman: Intellect gives much less Attack Power (Mental Dexterity 100% at 3/3 instead of 150%) and Stormstrike hits for less, less often. Strength and Attack Power gear now beat Intellect. Weapon imbues and totems also work together now, so Windfury Weapon and Windfury Totem both proc.
  • Affliction Warlock: Wrack hits 30% softer but makes your Corruption, Siphon Life and Bane of Agony tick twice as fast while you channel. Put all three up first, then Wrack. Soul Siphon only counts those three DoTs now.
  • Hunter: pets keep 115% of normal health (they had 150%). Arcane Shot and Summon Hawk cost 11% of base mana at every rank, and the Hawk hits about 80% harder.
  • Warlock pets: Imp, Voidwalker, Incubus and Succubus have about 15% more health, and the Imp's first 4 Firebolt ranks hit harder.
